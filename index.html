Infinity monitoring & consultancy

Company Description: Infinity Monitoring & Consultancy is a technology and consulting company that focuses on monitoring and optimizing building systems. Using data analytics, sensors, and smart software, the company helps organizations improve energy efficiency, detect technical issues early, and maintain a healthy indoor climate in buildings.

Tasks & Responsibilities: During my internship at Infinity Monitoring & Consultancy, I develop client-specific views in the SkySpark platform to help visualize and analyze building performance data. These views enable clients to monitor building systems, identify inefficiencies, and gain insights into energy consumption and system behavior. To build these views, I currently use Axon, SkySpark’s query and scripting language, to retrieve and process time-series data from building systems. I use this data to create dashboards and visualizations that present information in a clear and actionable way for clients. In addition, I am learning Fantom, the programming language underlying SkySpark. In the near future, I will use Fantom to develop and extend views, allowing for more advanced customization and functionality within the platform.
